Posting and credit

The few rules that keep the channel growing and the streamers happy.

Posting

  • Post at the same hour each time.
  • Start at 6 pm US Eastern, which is midnight in central Europe, when NA and EU players are both online. Move it once the analytics show when your viewers watch.
  • The same file on all three, uploaded natively. Never put a TikTok download, with its watermark, on Reels or Shorts.

Credit and rights

  • Every clip credits its streamer on screen and in the text.
  • Skip clips with music playing on stream: Content ID claims them and TikTok mutes them.
  • Message bigger streamers once to ask if they're fine with their clips on the channel, credited. Most say yes.
  • A takedown request is done within 24 hours, no argument.
  • YouTube can refuse monetisation for reused content. The captions, the hook and the credit help; they don't guarantee it.
