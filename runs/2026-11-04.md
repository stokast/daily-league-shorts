## What the checks showed

- The music verdict (any 10 s window at 0.3 or more) flagged four of the five picks. Scored every 3 s, their music
  rises only in the fight (0.4-0.6 with "Smash, crash", "Burst, pop", the multikill announcer) and falls to
  0.01-0.08 when it's over: game sound. The one real song (BigNoseMikey) stayed at 0.5-0.67 in every window.
- The most viewed English clips were talk: of the top 20 English, only Baus's Neeko play works without context.
  Further down, titles that name a play ("unc still got it", "need replay", a champion's name) were the ones worth
  checking; clips with no words at all can be the cleanest picks (Yone, Viper).
