## What the checks showed

- Music: none of the 16 had any (0.00-0.02). Two songs mixed under a real clip were caught down to 24 dB under the
  stream (0.35-0.51), so the verdict flags any 10 s window at 0.3 or more.
- Webcam: found where there was one (bottom right on most, bottom left for Baus, top right in Alois's queue layout);
  RATIRL's rat picture is correctly not a face.
- Whisper (small) guesses the language of short shouts wrong (midbeast's English came out Portuguese, NattyNatt's
  French); Twitch's stream language is the one to trust.
- Several streams burn their own live captions into the picture (NattyNatt, Alois, RATIRL, Nickich): the short's
  captions will need to sit clear of them or replace that strip.
